Materials for Future Technologies
(MFT Conference 2026)

Promoting interdisciplinary research and collaboration focused on developing next-generation materials enabling future technologies, sustainable development, and industrial innovation.

17–19 December 2026

Advanced & Functional Materials

  • Nanomaterials and Nanotechnology
  • Smart and Functional Materials
  • Biomaterials and Healthcare Materials
  • Polymer Science and Engineering
  • Composite and Hybrid Materials

  • Energy Storage Materials (Batteries & Supercapacitors)
  • Renewable Energy Materials
  • Semiconductor and Electronic Materials
  • Photonic and Optoelectronic Materials

Manufacturing & Industrial Materials

  • Additive Manufacturing Materials
  • Advanced Processing Techniques
  • Surface Engineering and Coatings

  • Materials for Smart Infrastructure & Mobility

Paper Submission Guidelines 📄

  • Original, unpublished research papers are invited.
  • All submissions will undergo double-blind peer review.
  • Accepted papers will be included in conference proceedings.
  • Selected papers may be recommended for indexed journals (Scopus/WoS – under process).
